Fathom Deeper

Privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026

Fathom Deeper is a science video channel. This policy covers this website and the two small apps the channel uses to publish its own videos: Fathom Deeper Uploader (Google and YouTube) and Fathom Deeper Publisher (Meta and Instagram). Only the channel's owner uses these apps, and only on the channel's own accounts. They are not offered to anyone else.

What the apps access

  • YouTube, through YouTube API Services: uploading and scheduling our videos, setting their titles, descriptions, playlists and captions, and reading our own channel's analytics.
  • Instagram, through Meta's Instagram API: publishing our Reels, reading their insights, and reading the comments on our posts so we can hide spam.
  • TikTok and Bluesky posts go out through Buffer, a scheduling service with its own privacy policy. We read replies to our Bluesky posts through Bluesky's public API.

Information about other people

When someone comments on or replies to one of our posts, our tools can read that public comment and the commenter's username. We use it only to moderate: hiding clear spam and blocking spam accounts, with each action logged. We never send automated replies. We don't collect anything else about viewers: no email addresses, no contact lists, no tracking.

How we use it

To publish our videos, to see how they perform, and to keep our comment sections free of spam. Nothing is sold, rented or used for advertising.

Where it's kept and for how long

  • On the channel owner's computer: the apps' sign-in tokens, our analytics records and the moderation log.
  • On Cloudflare: the Instagram sign-in token and the posting schedule, and each video while it waits to be published. Staged videos are deleted automatically about 30 days after upload.
  • Moderation log entries are kept no longer than 12 months.

Information leaves our hands only to reach the platforms themselves and the service providers named here, so they can do the posting.

Google and YouTube

Fathom Deeper Uploader uses YouTube API Services. Anyone using it agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles its side of the data under the Google Privacy Policy. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account third-party connections page.

Meta and Instagram

Access for Fathom Deeper Publisher can be removed at any time in Instagram under Settings, Apps and websites.

Deleting your information

To have anything we hold about you deleted, for example one of your comments in our moderation log, email fathomdeeper.ocean@gmail.com with your username and the post it was on. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. Editing or deleting a comment on the platform itself is done in that platform's app.

This website

This site is a set of plain pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. GitHub may log visits, such as IP addresses, for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Our videos and apps are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: fathomdeeper.ocean@gmail.com
