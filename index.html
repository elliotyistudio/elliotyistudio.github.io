FATHOM DEEPER

The deep ocean,

Fathom Deeper is about exploring and understanding the depths of worlds beneath the surface.

Short videos that take you down one stop at a time: the ocean's midnight zone, the floor of the Challenger Deep, the deepest hole ever drilled, and the giants of the ancient sea. Every number on screen comes with its source. The scenes are painted AI reconstructions, and each one is labeled.
